# Patnai AI & Engineering — Open Research

Welcome to the open-source research and documentation repository for **[Patnai.com](https://patnai.com)**.

## What is Patnai?
Patnai is an enterprise engineering firm specializing in deterministic AI architectures, custom autonomous agents, and high-performance workflow automation. We build the foundational systems that allow businesses to instantly qualify leads, generate automated quotations, and deploy sovereign, locally-hosted LLM environments.

## How Patnai Helps Businesses
We bridge the gap between abstract AI capabilities and actual business ROI. By engineering highly specialized systems—from 2-second WhatsApp CRM integrations to complex RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architectures—we eliminate manual bottlenecks. Our systems operate 24/7 without the monthly overhead of generic third-party SaaS wrappers.

## Purpose of this Repository
This repository serves as the public knowledge base and technical hub for Patnai. It contains:
- **Technical Research**: Deep dives into the latency, security, and infrastructure of deploying production-grade AI.
- **RAG Architecture**: Blueprints and evaluations of Retrieval-Augmented Generation using vector databases (like Qdrant) and FastApi.
- **AI Agents**: Case studies and engineering documentation on how to build deterministic, goal-oriented AI agents.
- **Automation Protocols**: Open guidelines for integrating secure WhatsApp webhooks, instant PDF generation, and cross-platform synchronization.

We believe that sharing our findings on infrastructure costs, performance benchmarks, and retrieval algorithms helps push the entire Indian AI ecosystem forward.
